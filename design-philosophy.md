# Rounded Wanderer

Soft geometry as emotional language. Forms that feel held in the palm — every curve intentionally generous, every edge forgiven. The visual vocabulary of comfort: circles within circles, proportions that recall early childhood drawings, shapes that need no explanation because they speak directly to something pre-verbal.

Color as warmth, not decoration. A restrained triad — deep ink black, cloud white, and a single accent of baked earth orange. The black and white provide the grounding, the structure, the quiet confidence. The orange arrives sparingly — a hat brim, a strap, a sun-warmed detail — never shouting, always placing a gentle hand on the shoulder. This is not a palette that needs to convince; it simply arrives and belongs.

Materiality through digital craft. Forms are rendered with the weight of hand-painted ceramics — subtle grain, imperfectly perfect edges, surfaces that absorb light rather than reflect it. Every shape feels as though it could be lifted from the screen and cupped in two hands. The craftsmanship shows in restraint: knowing when a highlight is enough, when a shadow tells the whole story.

Character as companion, not mascot. The figure occupies space with gentle confidence — not performing, not pleading for attention, simply present. Its posture says "I'm here with you." Its eyes meet the viewer with quiet warmth. This is not a logo; this is a travel companion, a familiar face that greets you each morning of your journey.

Composition breathes. The figure floats in generous negative space, anchored by nothing but its own weight. No background competes. No frame constrains. The isolation is intentional — this character exists to be placed anywhere, to live on any surface, to belong wherever the journey takes it.

Every pixel chosen with care. This is the work of countless refinements, of decisions made and remade, of elements added and then removed until only the essential remains. The result appears effortless, which is the highest form of craft.
